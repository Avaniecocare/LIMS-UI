The LIMS works in your phone's browser, with a layout made for small screens. Technicians, approvers, dispatch and accounts can do their everyday work away from a desk. A few screens with long forms or wide tables are better on a computer.

The phone layout applies to screens narrower than 768 pixels. Tablets get the full app with touch-sized controls. What you may do is the same on every device; only the layout changes.

## The bottom bar and More

On a phone the menu is a bar at the bottom of the screen. It holds up to four of your most useful pages, in this order of preference, then **More**:

**Dashboard**, **My tests**, **Approve**, **Samples**, **Dues** (Due Invoices), **Invoices**, **Dispatch**, **Customers**, **Quality**, **Orders**.

You see only the pages your role allows. Pages that are best on a computer are never put in the bar.

Select **More** to open the full menu as a drawer. Pages that are best on a computer show a small monitor icon. Select a page to go there, or tap outside the drawer to close it.

## The top bar

- Select the logo to go to the **Dashboard**.
- Select the search icon (**Search or scan**) to open a full-width search box. Select **Cancel** to close it.
- Select the bell for **Notifications**.
- Select your initials for the account menu: **My account**, **Appearance** and **Sign out**.

## The dashboard on a phone

At the top of the **Dashboard** you get quick actions for the things people do on the move, shown only if your role allows them:

- **Scan a label** (where the phone can scan; see below)
- **My tests**
- **To approve**
- **Dispatch**
- **Due invoices**

The **Date range** is folded away under its own heading; open it to change the period. **My work** and the summary tiles follow.

## Cards instead of tables

Wide tables become a list of cards on a phone. Each card shows the first four fields as label and value. Select **More details** to see the rest, and **Fewer details** to fold them again. Select a card to open the record, as you would select a row on a computer.

## The Filters sheet

On a list with filters, a **Filters** button sits next to the search box. A number on it shows how many filters are in use.

1. Select **Filters**.
2. Change the filters. They apply as you change them.
3. Select **Show results**, or **Clear filters** to remove them all.

## The actions bar

A page's main buttons, such as **Submit results**, **Approve and sign** or **Record payment**, sit in a bar just above the bottom bar, within reach of your thumb. If there are more buttons than fit, slide the bar sideways.

Where the phone supports it, a **Share** button next to the page title shares the page's link through the phone's own share sheet.

## How to scan a label with the camera

**Goal**: Open a sample, job or report by pointing the phone's camera at its label.

**Who can do it**: Everyone. You open only what your role allows; a technician who scans a sample goes to their own test on it.

**Before you start**: The **Scan a label** button appears only where the browser can read codes from the camera, for example Chrome on Android. Elsewhere, type the number in the search box instead.

**Steps**:

1. Open the **Dashboard**.
2. Select **Scan a label**.
3. If the phone asks, allow camera access for the LIMS.
4. Point the camera at the label's QR code or barcode.

**Result**: The message "Found …. Opening…" appears and the record opens. If nothing matches, the dialog says "Nothing matches …. Try another label." and keeps scanning.

If the dialog says "The camera could not be opened", allow camera access for the LIMS address in the browser's site settings and try again.

## How to send photos from a phone to a record on a computer

**Goal**: Add photos to a sample, a test or a booking, or pages of a customer letter, using your phone's camera while you work on the computer.

**Who can do it**: Anyone who can add photos to that record. The photos-from-a-phone module must be on.

**Steps**:

1. On the computer, select **Use phone**: next to a sample's images on the job page, a test's **Result images**, the photos on the booking form, or the captured pages of a **Customer letter**.
2. A dialog **Send photos from your phone** shows a QR code.
3. Scan the code with the phone's camera. A camera page opens for that record only; no sign-in is needed on the phone.
4. On the phone, select **Take photo** or **Choose from gallery**.
5. On the computer, wait for "… photo(s) received from the phone", then select **Done**.

**Result**: The photos are added to the record. The code stops working when you close the dialog or when its time runs out; select **Make a new code** for another.

**Related**: [Phone upload](/help/features/phone-upload/)

## How to install the LIMS as an app

**Goal**: Put the LIMS on your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

**Who can do it**: Everyone.

**Steps**:

1. Open the LIMS address in your phone's browser and sign in.
2. On Android with Chrome, open the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. On an iPhone or iPad with Safari, select the Share button and choose **Add to Home Screen**.

**Result**: The LIMS icon appears on your home screen and opens in its own window without the browser's address bar.

In the browser, without installing, the header and the bottom bar stay in place while you scroll, and the browser hides its own address and tool bars as you scroll down, so the page uses the whole screen. Scroll up a little to bring them back.

:::note
The installed app still needs a connection. No lab data is stored on the phone. Without a connection you see "You are offline"; select **Try again** when you are back online.
:::

The menu names in steps 2 and 3 belong to the browser and can differ between versions.

## Screens that are best on a larger screen

Some screens have more than a phone can show comfortably. On a phone they first show **Best on a larger screen**, with a sentence on what the screen is for:

| Screen | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Book samples** | Booking takes the customer, every sample, its tests and deviations in one long form. |
| **New quotation** and editing a quotation | A quotation is a table of tests, quantities, rates and discounts. |
| **Planning** | Planning assigns many tests at once against each analyst's and instrument's workload. |
| **Masters** | Masters hold the test catalogue, specifications, rates and imports. |
| **Admin** | Admin covers users, roles, settings, branding, templates and the audit log. |
| Lab setup | Setting up the laboratory walks through its profile, branding and catalogue. |
| **Analytics** | Analytics shows charts across operations, quality, people and money. |
| **Reports** (MIS) | MIS reports are wide tables with Excel and PDF export. |

On that page you can:

- **Email me the link**: opens your email app with a message to yourself containing the page's link, to open later on a computer.
- **Copy link**: copies the link.
- **Continue anyway**: opens the full screen on the phone. The choice lasts for this visit.

These screens work fully on a phone: **Dashboard**, **My Tests** and each test, **Approvals** and each report, and **My account**. All other screens show their key fields and main actions.
