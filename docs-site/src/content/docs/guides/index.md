Each guide describes a typical day for one role and the tasks that role does, step by step. Your role is shown under your name when you select it at the top right of the LIMS. If you hold several roles, read each guide.

These are the roles a new installation starts with. Your administrator may have renamed them, changed what they allow, or added others. What you can do is decided by the permissions your roles hold, so if a button in a guide is missing for you, ask your administrator.

| Role | What they do | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Sets up users, roles, masters, settings and branding; reads the audit log; can see all records | [Admin guide](/help/guides/admin/) |
| Sales / Business Development | Keeps customers, records enquiries as orders, and sends and follows up quotations | [Sales guide](/help/guides/sales/) |
| Sample Reception | Books jobs and samples, records deviations, prints labels and the SRF, sends acknowledgements | [Sample Reception guide](/help/guides/sample-reception/) |
| Lab Manager / Planner | Plans tests against specifications, assigns technicians and instruments, prints job cards, watches workload | [Lab Manager / Planner guide](/help/guides/lab-manager-planner/) |
| Technician / Analyst | Works on assigned tests: stages, results, images and worksheets | [Technician / Analyst guide](/help/guides/technician-analyst/) |
| Head of Department | Approves or sends back test results | [Head of Department guide](/help/guides/head-of-department/) |
| Report Reviewer | Checks and corrects draft reports and passes them for signing | [Report Reviewer guide](/help/guides/report-reviewer/) |
| Authorised Signatory | Signs reports electronically, issues and emails them, starts revisions | [Authorised Signatory guide](/help/guides/authorised-signatory/) |
| Dispatch | Records dispatches and challans, and tracks returnable samples | [Dispatch guide](/help/guides/dispatch/) |
| Accounts | Raises invoices, records payments and credit notes, chases due invoices, keeps rates | [Accounts guide](/help/guides/accounts/) |
| Management | Reads dashboards, analytics and MIS reports; views records without changing them | [Management guide](/help/guides/management/) |
| Quality Manager | Keeps equipment calibrated, authorises staff, works non-conformances and CAPA | [Quality Manager guide](/help/guides/quality-manager/) |
| Document Controller | Runs the file library: folders, uploads and controlled documents | [Document Controller guide](/help/guides/document-controller/) |

Working away from a desk? Read [Using the LIMS on a phone](/help/guides/on-a-phone/).
