This page walks you through the parts of the LIMS everyone uses, whatever their role. It takes about ten minutes.

## Sign in

**Goal**: Open the LIMS with your own account.

**Who can do it**: Everyone with an account. Your administrator creates it.

**Before you start**: You need the address of your lab's LIMS (for example `https://lims.example.com`), your email address and a password.

**Steps**:

1. Open the LIMS address in your browser.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.
4. If two-factor login is on for your account, enter the **6-digit code from your authenticator app** and select **Verify**.
5. If your administrator set a temporary password, the page **Choose a new password** opens. Enter the **Current password**, a **New password** and **Repeat new password**, then select **Change password**. Sign in again with the new password.

**Result**: The **Dashboard** opens, with "Welcome" and your name. If you followed a link from an email or notification, you go straight to that page instead.

A password needs at least 10 characters, with upper and lower case, a digit and a symbol.

:::tip
Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, enter your **Email** and select **Send reset link**. The link in the email works for one hour.
:::

## The dashboard and "My work"

The **Dashboard** is your starting page. At the top, **My work** lists what is waiting on you, with a count and a link for each item. You see only the items your role covers, and only those with something waiting:

| Item | Who sees it |
| --- | --- |
| **Tests assigned to me**, **My tests past due**, **Sent back to me** | Technicians |
| **Results to approve** | Heads of department |
| **Reports to review** | Report reviewers |
| **Reports to sign**, **Reports to issue** | Authorised signatories |
| **Tests to plan**, **Tests to assign** | Lab managers and planners |
| **Draft quotations** | Sales |
| **Jobs ready to invoice**, **Overdue invoices** | Accounts |

When nothing is waiting you see "Nothing is waiting on you right now."

Below **My work**, depending on your role, you see summary tiles for **Operations**, **Quality** and **Commercial**, and **Calibrations due**. Select a tile to open the report behind it. Change the **Period** to see another date range. If you can see charts, **Open analytics** takes you to them.

## The menu

The menu runs down the left side. It shows only the pages your role allows and the modules your lab has turned on. Select the menu button at the top (**Open menu**) to show the names next to the icons; select it again (**Close menu**) to fold it back.

On a phone the menu is a bar at the bottom of the screen with your most-used pages and **More**. See [Using the LIMS on a phone](/help/guides/on-a-phone/).

If you open a page your role does not include, you see "You don't have access to this page". Write **Why do you need it?** and select **Request access**. An administrator answers, and you get a notification either way.

## Search and scan

**Goal**: Jump to any job, sample, report, invoice or customer by its number or name.

**Who can do it**: Everyone. You only find records you are allowed to see.

**Steps**:

1. Select the search box at the top of the page (**Search or scan a job, sample, report, invoice or customer**), or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘+K** on a Mac). You can also press **/** when you are not typing in a field.
2. Type at least two characters of a number or name.
3. Use the arrow keys to choose a result and press **Enter**, or select it.

**Result**: The record opens. If you type a complete number and press **Enter**, it opens directly.

A barcode scanner that types like a keyboard works from any screen: scan a sample label and the sample opens. A technician who scans a sample goes to their own test on it. On a phone you can also scan a label with the camera. See [Search and scanning](/help/features/search-scanning/).

## Notifications

The bell at the top right shows how many notifications you have not read. Select it to see the latest 20. Select a notification's title to open the record; it is marked read. Use **Mark read** on one, or **Mark all read**.

Notifications tell you, for example, that tests were assigned to you, work was sent back, approvals are waiting, a TAT is near or crossed, or an access request was answered. You always see them in the bell. You choose which are also emailed to you in **My account**.

**Related**: [Notifications](/help/features/notifications/)

## My account

Select your name at the top right, then **My account**. There you can:

- **Change password**: enter the **Current password** and a **New password**, then select **Change password**. You are signed out and sign in again with the new password.
- **Two-factor login**: set up an authenticator app. This is offered for Admin, Authorised Signatory and Accounts roles.
- **Email notifications**: tick the notifications you also want by email.
- **Recent sign-ins**: your last 50 sign-ins and failed attempts.
- **Where you're signed in**: every browser signed in to your account. Select **Sign out** next to one, or **Sign out everywhere else**.
- **My access requests** and **Problems I reported**: the progress of anything you asked for or reported.

**Related**: [My account](/help/features/account/)

## Light or dark

**Goal**: Choose how the LIMS looks.

**Who can do it**: Everyone.

**Steps**:

1. Select your name at the top right.
2. Under **Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as this device**.

**Result**: The screen changes at once. **Same as this device** follows your computer's or phone's own setting. The choice is remembered by this browser, so set it again on another computer or phone.

## Sign out

**Goal**: End your session on this browser.

**Who can do it**: Everyone.

**Steps**:

1. Select your name at the top right.
2. Select **Sign out**.

**Result**: The sign-in page opens.

:::caution
On a shared computer, always sign out when you leave. To end sessions on other devices, use **Sign out everywhere else** in **My account**.
:::
