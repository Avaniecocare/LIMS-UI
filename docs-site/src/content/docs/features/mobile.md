The system runs in the phone's browser; there is nothing to download from an app store. Any screen narrower than 768 pixels gets the phone layout. Tablets get the full app, with larger touch targets. Your role decides what you can do, the same as on a computer.

For a task-by-task guide, see [Using the system on a phone](/help/guides/on-a-phone/).

<!-- screenshot: mobile-dashboard -->

## The phone layout

- **Bottom bar:** up to four of the places most useful to you, picked in this order from what your role can open: Dashboard, My tests, Approve, Samples, Dues (Due Invoices), Invoices, Dispatch, Customers, Quality, Orders. The last button, **More**, opens the full menu.
- **Search or scan:** the magnifier at the top opens the search bar. A barcode scanner works from any screen. See [Search and scanning](/help/features/search-scanning/).
- **Dashboard:** starts with **Scan a label** (the phone camera reads a label) and quick links (My tests, To approve, Dispatch, Due invoices, for the roles that have them). The date range sits under **Date range**.
- **Lists:** each row becomes a card showing its first four fields; the rest are under **More details**. Filters move behind a **Filters** button that counts the ones in use.
- **Account menu:** your initials at the top right open **My account**, **Appearance** and **Sign out**.

## Screens best on a computer

These screens are wide or long by nature. On a phone they first show **Best on a larger screen**, with what the screen is for and three choices: **Email me the link**, **Copy link**, or **Continue anyway** (opens the full screen for the rest of the visit). They are marked with a monitor icon in the **More** menu and never appear in the bottom bar.

| Screen | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Book samples | The customer, every sample, its tests and deviations in one long form |
| New or edit quotation | A table of tests, quantities, rates and discounts |
| Planning | Assigning many tests at once against each analyst's and instrument's workload |
| Masters | The test catalogue, specifications, rates and imports |
| Admin, and the lab setup wizard | Users, roles, settings, branding, templates and the audit log |
| Analytics | Charts across operations, quality, people and money |
| Reports (MIS) | Wide tables with Excel and PDF export |

The Dashboard, **My Tests** and test pages, **Approvals** and report pages, and **My account** are built to work fully on a phone. Other screens show their key fields and main actions.

## Photos on a phone

On a phone you add photos with the phone's own camera and files (**Use camera**, **Add file**). The **Use phone** QR code is only offered on a computer. See [Send photos from your phone](/help/features/phone-upload/).

## Install it as an app

You can add the system to the phone's home screen. It then opens full-screen, without the browser's address bar, like an installed app.

**Steps**

1. Open the system's address in the phone's browser and sign in.
2. Android (Chrome): open the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new icon.

The name under the icon is the lab's product name when the server sets it (`APP_NAME`, taken from `BRAND_NAME` in a LIMS-Deploy install); otherwise it is "Laboratory".

The installed app keeps nothing on the phone: pages and data always come from the server, and a new release is picked up the next time it opens. Without a connection it shows **You are offline** instead of a browser error. Nothing you saved is lost.
